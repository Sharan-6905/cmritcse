04 / Architecture

What happens to your photo

Detect

Gemini vision finds each item and draws a box.

Classify

Each crop goes through the trained ResNet50 (with a Grad-CAM heatmap).

Verify

Gemini checks the CNN against the photo and overrides it when it disagrees.

Advise

A knowledge base gives the bin, the steps and the safety warnings.

Optimize

Plain code compares “as-is” with “after prep”: recovered share and CO₂.

05 / Limits

What to keep in mind

  • The CNN was trained on about 2.5k clean photos from one dataset, so cluttered real-world photos score lower.
  • Item names, boxes and the final check depend on the Gemini API, which has daily limits on the free tier.
  • CO₂ and recovery figures use typical item weights and simple contamination rules: estimates, not measurements.
  • Hazardous items were checked on only a handful of photos, and textiles on none.
  • Disposal advice is general. Local rules differ, so check your own council or hazardous-waste service.
